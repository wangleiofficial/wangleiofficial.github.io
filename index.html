Lei Wang

Associate Research Professor · Bioinformatics

EN / 中文
I am an Associate Research Professor at Central China Normal University and a member of Professor Guangfu Yang’s group. I received my PhD in Bioinformatics Engineering from Huazhong University of Science and Technology in December 2023, and completed postdoctoral research at its School of Computer Science and Technology from 2024 to July 2026 with iSyslab. I work at the intersection of artificial intelligence and bioinformatics, focusing on protein language models, sequence and structure analysis, RNA structure prediction, peptide therapeutics, and bioinformatics tools.

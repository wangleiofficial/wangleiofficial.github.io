Our paper, <em>ProtFormer-Site: Ultra-fast and Accurate Prediction of Protein-Protein Interaction Sites with Protein Language Model</em>, has been published in <em>Interdisciplinary Sciences: Computational Life Sciences</em>.

ProtFormer-Site is a protein-language-model-based method for protein-protein interaction site prediction, designed to provide both high accuracy and efficient inference.

Read the paper: <a href="https://doi.org/10.1007/s12539-026-00848-1" target="_blank" rel="noopener">https://doi.org/10.1007/s12539-026-00848-1</a>

Our paper, <em>SSAlign: Fast and Sensitive Protein Structure Search at Scale</em>, is now available online as an accepted manuscript in <em>Bioinformatics</em>.

SSAlign combines protein sequence–structure embeddings with dense retrieval and alignment refinement to support fast, sensitive searches across large protein structure databases.

Authors: Lei Wang, Xuchao Zhang, Yan Wang, and Zhidong Xue. Lei Wang and Xuchao Zhang contributed equally to this work.

DOI: [10.1093/bioinformatics/btag789](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btag789).

### 中文简介

论文 <em>SSAlign: Fast and Sensitive Protein Structure Search at Scale</em> 已以 accepted manuscript 形式在线发表于 <em>Bioinformatics</em>。

SSAlign 将蛋白质序列与结构联合表征、稠密检索和比对精化相结合，实现面向大规模蛋白质结构数据库的快速、高灵敏度检索。

作者：Lei Wang、Xuchao Zhang、Yan Wang 和 Zhidong Xue。其中 Lei Wang 与 Xuchao Zhang 为共同第一作者。

DOI：[10.1093/bioinformatics/btag789](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btag789)。
